# Iota-and-the-Tsunami_Story

This project is a website featuring an interactive story called “Iota and the Tsunami.” It is
made up of three files: home, about, and play. These files give the plurpose of the site and 
acess to the story. The story follows a young girl named Iota who tries to save her vilage 
from an incomming tusunami. 

Throughout the story, users are given the ability to determine many of her actions throughout
the reading. This give users a deeper experience into the story where they benefit or suffer
from their choices.

## Purpose

The purpos of this project is to deliver a message to the audience. I find stories to be an 
effective meothod in learning principles. The story revolves around the power of unity and 
taking action after warnings. The audience are readers and video game players aged 10 to 20. 
This mixure of a video game and book of this project makes this group interested.
