Project Overview

This site features “Iota and the Tsunami,” an interactive story about a girl named Iota who attempts to save her village from an approaching tsunami. Users are given the ability to determine many of Iota’s decisions in the narrative, allowing them to experience the consequences or benefits of their choices in a engaging and adventurous story.

Project Purpose and Audience

This project is important to me because it provides valuable principles. I have found stories to be a powerful way to learn, which is why I find this project important. It focuses on teaching unity and listening to warnings. The audience are readers and video game players from 10 to 20 because of the story's mix of a game and book.
