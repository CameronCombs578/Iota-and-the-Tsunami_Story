About

Below is a detailed look into the reason for the site:

My project will be a website featuring an interactive story called “Iota and the Tsunami.” The story follows a young girl named Iota who, after being warned of an approaching tsunami, tries to save her village. In the story, users experience being placed in Iota’s mission by playing a unique and essential role in determining her actions. This project matters because of its lessons. I believe principles are best learned through stories, making this site valuable for learning. The story centers on two main themes: the power of unity and taking action after warnings. My audience includes readers and video game players aged 10 to 20. This group would enjoy the project because of the mix of video games and books on the site.
